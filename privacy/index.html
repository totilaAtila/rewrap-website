Privacy Policy

Rewrap · last updated 26 July 2026

Short version: Rewrap collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and the app has no internet permission. Everything you read and every note you make stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Rewrap does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. We operate no server and run no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Your documents — opened only through Android's document picker; Rewrap accesses just the files you choose.
  • Your reading data — positions, bookmarks, highlights, per-book settings and the on-device text cache.
  • App settings — themes, fonts and preferences.

All of this is stored locally in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app (unless you turn on the optional backup below).

Optional cloud backup

Rewrap includes a setting — off by default — that lets Android's own Auto Backup include the app's data in your device's Google backup, so your library and notes can return after a reinstall. This uses Google's backup service under your Google account and Google's terms; Rewrap itself still has no server and receives none of your data. Turn it off and uninstalling leaves nothing behind.

Permissions

Rewrap requests no internet permission. The permissions it does use are only for on-device features: a foreground service and wake lock so Read Aloud can continue with the screen off, and notifications for the lock-screen playback controls. None of these collect or send data.

Text recognition (scans)

Scanned pages are recognized entirely on your device using on-device machine-learning models bundled in the app. Page images are never uploaded.

Children

Rewrap is safe for all ages: it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at the developer contact email shown on our Google Play listing.
