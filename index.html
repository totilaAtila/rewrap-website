Reflow without breaking the page

Not classic zoom (which forces sideways scrolling) and not full reflow (which destroys the layout). Text grows and rewraps in place; images, tables and figures stay where they belong and scale with the font.

🔍

Scans read on the phone

Scanned PDFs and photos of pages are recognized right on the device — no upload. Angled photos are straightened, faint scans enhanced, and the app repairs recognition errors it can prove from the book itself.

Truly private

Zero cloud, zero account, zero network permissions. Your books, your notes and your reading positions never leave your phone. A one-time purchase — no subscription.

Everything it does

Built feature by feature, measured on real books and real devices.

Faithful text

Digital PDFs read exactly from the file's text layer — any language. Scans go through on-device recognition with evidence-based repairs (broken fonts, lost diacritics, phantom accents, end-of-line hyphens). Fix any word by tapping it.

Formats

PDF, EPUB, TXT and photos — a single photo, a whole folder as one book, or several bound into a PDF. Scan pages with the camera, split double-page scans, straighten angled shots.

Read aloud

Natural text-to-speech in the book's own language — choose engine, voice and speed, follow the spoken word on the page, and keep listening with the screen off, from the lock screen or your headphones.

Notes & highlights

Highlight passages in every format (they stay anchored even when you change the font), then export your notes as text, Markdown, Anki cards or PDF — one tap, straight to the share sheet.

Navigate

Full-text search (accents don't matter), bookmarks, a table of contents (the book's own or one built from headings), tap-to-define with your offline dictionary, and a remembered position per book.

Export & convert

Turn any book into EPUB, TXT, DOCX or PDF — real chapters, cover and cleaned text. Convert a TXT into a proper EPUB, or bind photos into a single PDF.

Comfort

Light, Sepia, Night, pure-black AMOLED and custom themes; line/letter spacing; a reading ruler; bionic bold; and a dyslexia-friendly font that works even on scanned books — because the text is re-typeset.

Safety nets

Automatic on-device backups, optional cloud backup you control, portable export/import of your reading data, and one-tap restore — plus the original page is always a long-press away.

The reader that does the hard part locally

Reflowing a scanned book, recognizing its text, repairing it, reading it aloud and exporting it — all on your phone, the things other readers send to the cloud or simply refuse to do.
